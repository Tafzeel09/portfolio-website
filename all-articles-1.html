Salah
The Importance of Namaz (Salah)
May 2026 · By Taiba Shabbir
إِنَّ الصَّلَاةَ كَانَتْ عَلَى الْمُؤْمِنِينَ كِتَابًا مَّوْقُوتًا
Inna as-salaata kaanat 'alal-mu'mineena kitaabam-mawqootaa
"Indeed, prayer has been decreed upon the believers a decree of specified times." — Surah An-Nisa 4:103

Salah — the Islamic prayer — is the second pillar of Islam and the most beloved act of worship in the sight of Allah ﷻ. It is the direct connection between a servant and his Creator, a gift given to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ during the night of Al-Isra wal Mi'raj.

🕌 Why is Salah So Important?

Salah was made obligatory not as a burden, but as a mercy. It is a pause from the distractions of this world — five times a day, we turn our hearts and faces toward Allah and remember the purpose of our existence.

💬 The Prophet ﷺ said: "The first matter that the servant will be brought to account for on the Day of Judgment is the prayer. If it is sound, then the rest of his deeds will be sound. And if it is incomplete, then the rest of his deeds will be incomplete." — (At-Tabarani)

📖 Salah in the Quran

Allah ﷻ mentions Salah over 90 times in the Quran, often paired with Zakat, showing its supreme importance. It is not just a ritual — it is the foundation of a Muslim's life.

وَأَقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَآتُوا الزَّكَاةَ وَارْكَعُوا مَعَ الرَّاكِعِينَ
Wa aqeemus-salaata wa aatuz-zakaata war-ka'oo ma'ar-raaki'een
"And establish prayer and give zakah and bow with those who bow." — Surah Al-Baqarah 2:43

✨ Benefits of Salah

1. Purification from sins: The Prophet ﷺ compared the five daily prayers to a river flowing in front of your house — bathing in it five times a day leaves no dirt behind.

2. Protection from evil: Allah says in the Quran — "Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing." (29:45)

3. Peace of the heart: In every Salah, we recite Al-Fatiha — a conversation with Allah — and find tranquility that nothing in the dunya can give.

4. Discipline and structure: Five prayers at specific times teach a Muslim discipline, time-management, and constant God-consciousness (Taqwa).

🌙 Remember: Missing Salah without a valid excuse is a major sin. But the door of tawbah (repentance) is always open. Start today — even if you have missed prayers in the past. Allah loves those who return to Him.

The Five Daily Prayers — Arabic & English
May 2026 · By Taiba Shabbir

Allah ﷻ has prescribed five prayers upon every adult Muslim each day. Each prayer has a specific time, a specific number of Rakaats, and holds its own spiritual beauty. Here is your complete guide:

🌅 Fajr Dawn — Before Sunrise
2 Rakaats Fard
الصَّلَاةُ خَيْرٌ مِنَ النَّوْمِ
As-salaatu khayrum minan-nawm
"Prayer is better than sleep." — (Called in Fajr Adhan only)

Fajr is the prayer of the early morning — prayed before sunrise. It is one of the most rewarded prayers. The Prophet ﷺ said whoever prays Fajr is under the protection of Allah for the day.

☀️ Dhuhr After Midday
4 Rakaats Fard + 4 Sunnah before + 2 Sunnah after

Dhuhr is prayed after the sun passes its zenith. It is a moment to pause in the middle of the day and reconnect with Allah ﷻ amidst daily responsibilities.

🌤️ Asr Late Afternoon
4 Rakaats Fard
حَافِظُوا عَلَى الصَّلَوَاتِ وَالصَّلَاةِ الْوُسْطَىٰ
Haafizoo 'alas-salawaati was-salaatil-wustaa
"Maintain with care the [obligatory] prayers and [in particular] the middle prayer." — Surah Al-Baqarah 2:238

Many scholars consider Asr to be the "middle prayer" mentioned in the Quran. The Prophet ﷺ warned severely about missing this prayer.

🌇 Maghrib Just After Sunset
3 Rakaats Fard + 2 Sunnah after

Maghrib is prayed right after sunset — its time window is short, so it should not be delayed. It marks the end of the day and the beginning of the night.

🌙 Isha Night
4 Rakaats Fard + 2 Sunnah after + 3 Witr (Wajib)

Isha is the night prayer — the last of the five. It closes the day with remembrance of Allah. The Prophet ﷺ valued Isha and Fajr in congregation so highly that he compared them to praying the whole night.

🤲 The Opening of Every Prayer — Surah Al-Fatiha

Every Rakaat begins with Surah Al-Fatiha — the greatest Surah of the Quran:

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ﴿١﴾ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ﴿٢﴾ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ﴿٣﴾ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ ﴿٤﴾ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ﴿٥﴾ اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ ﴿٦﴾ صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ ﴿٧﴾
"In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, Lord of all the worlds. The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Master of the Day of Judgment. You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. Guide us to the straight path — the path of those You have blessed, not of those who have earned anger, nor of those who are astray."
Azkaar
Daily Masnoon Azkaar — Arabic with Translation
May 2026 · By Taiba Shabbir

Azkaar (pl. of Zikr) are the remembrances of Allah prescribed by our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ for specific moments of our day. Reciting them keeps our hearts connected to Allah and brings barakah into our lives.

أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ
Alaa bi-dhikrillahi tatma'innul-quloob
"Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest." — Surah Ar-Ra'd 13:28

🌅 Azkar-e-Subah — Morning Remembrances

أَصْبَحْنَا وَأَصْبَحَ الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ
Asbahna wa asbahal-mulku lillah, wal-hamdu lillah
"We have entered the morning, and sovereignty belongs to Allah, and all praise is for Allah." — (Abu Dawud)
اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ النُّشُورُ
Allahumma bika asbahna, wa bika amsayna, wa bika nahya, wa bika namootu, wa ilaykan-nushoor
"O Allah, by You we enter the morning, by You we enter the evening, by You we live, by You we die, and to You is the resurrection." — (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi)
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ
Subhaanallahi wa bihamdih
"Glory be to Allah and all praise is for Him." — Recite 100 times in the morning (Muslim)

🌙 Azkar-e-Masa — Evening Remembrances

أَمْسَيْنَا وَأَمْسَى الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ
Amsayna wa amsal-mulku lillah, wal-hamdu lillah
"We have entered the evening, and sovereignty belongs to Allah, and all praise is for Allah." — (Abu Dawud)
أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
A'oodhu bi-kalimaatillahit-taammaati min sharri maa khalaq
"I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created." — Recite 3 times in the evening (Muslim) — protects from harm

😴 Dua Before Sleeping

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا
Bismika Allahumma amootu wa ahya
"In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live." — (Bukhari)
اللَّهُمَّ قِنِي عَذَابَكَ يَوْمَ تَبْعَثُ عِبَادَكَ
Allahumma qinee 'adhaabaka yawma tab'athu 'ibaadak
"O Allah, protect me from Your punishment on the day You resurrect Your servants." — Recite 3 times (Abu Dawud)

🌄 Dua Upon Waking Up

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ
Alhamdu lillahil-ladhee ahyaana ba'da maa amaatana wa ilayhin-nushoor
"All praise is for Allah who gave us life after having given us death, and to Him is the resurrection." — (Bukhari)

🍽️ Dua Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
Bismillah
"In the name of Allah." — (Bukhari, Muslim)

✅ After Eating

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنِي هَذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّةٍ
Alhamdu lillahil-ladhee at'amanee haadha wa razaqaneehi min ghayri hawlim minnee wa laa quwwah
"All praise is for Allah who fed me this and provided it for me without any power or effort on my part." — (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi) — sins are forgiven

💡 Tip: Start with just ONE azkar a day — perhaps Bismillah before eating or the sleeping dua. Consistency in small deeds is more beloved to Allah than large deeds done occasionally. — (Bukhari)

Aqeedah
The Foundations of Islamic Belief
April 2026 · By Taiba Shabbir

Aqeedah means belief — the firm conviction of the heart. The six pillars of Iman are the foundation upon which every Muslim builds their faith and relationship with Allah ﷻ.

آمَنَ الرَّسُولُ بِمَا أُنزِلَ إِلَيْهِ مِن رَّبِّهِ وَالْمُؤْمِنُونَ ۚ كُلٌّ آمَنَ بِاللَّهِ وَمَلَائِكَتِهِ وَكُتُبِهِ وَرُسُلِهِ
"The Messenger has believed in what was revealed to him from his Lord, and [so have] the believers. All of them have believed in Allah, His angels, His books, and His messengers..." — Surah Al-Baqarah 2:285

The Six Pillars of Iman

1. Belief in Allah — Tawheed: that Allah is One, with no partners, no son, no equal.

2. Belief in His Angels — They are created from light, they do not disobey Allah, and they carry out His commands.

3. Belief in His Books — The Quran, Injeel, Tawrah, Zaboor — with the Quran being the final, preserved word of Allah.

4. Belief in His Messengers — From Adam ﷺ to Muhammad ﷺ, the seal of all prophets.

5. Belief in the Last Day — The Day of Judgment, paradise, and hellfire.

6. Belief in Qadar — That good and bad are from Allah's divine decree, and we accept both with trust in His wisdom.

🌟 Strong Aqeedah is the foundation — without it, no deed has lasting value. Protect your beliefs by learning, reflecting on the Quran, and keeping righteous company.

Tafsir
Reflections on Surah Al-Fatiha
March 2026 · By Taiba Shabbir

Surah Al-Fatiha — "The Opening" — is the greatest Surah of the Quran. We recite it at least 17 times a day in our prayers. Yet how deeply do we reflect on its meaning?

وَلَقَدْ آتَيْنَاكَ سَبْعًا مِّنَ الْمَثَانِي وَالْقُرْآنَ الْعَظِيمَ
"And We have certainly given you seven of the often-repeated [verses] and the great Quran." — Surah Al-Hijr 15:87 — referring to Al-Fatiha

Verse by Verse Reflection

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ — We begin every action in the name of Allah — the Most Gracious to all creation, the Most Merciful to the believers.

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ — All praise belongs to Allah alone — not because we earned something, but because He is worthy of all praise simply by being who He is.

مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ — He is the Master of the Day of Judgment. This reminds us that this dunya is temporary — accountability is coming.

إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ — "You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help." — This is the heart of Tawheed — total submission and total reliance on Allah alone.

اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ — The most important dua we make — 17 times a day — is to be guided to the straight path. If Allah answers this, everything else follows.

💎 The Prophet ﷺ called Al-Fatiha "Umm Al-Quran" — the Mother of the Quran. It contains the entire message of Islam: who Allah is, our relationship with Him, and what we need from Him.

Purifying the Heart: A Practical Guide
February 2026 · By Taiba Shabbir

The heart is the king of the body. When it is pure, the entire being is pure. When it is diseased, everything suffers. This is the foundation of Tazkiyah — the purification of the soul.

يَوْمَ لَا يَنفَعُ مَالٌ وَلَا بَنُونَ ﴿٨٨﴾ إِلَّا مَنْ أَتَى اللَّهَ بِقَلْبٍ سَلِيمٍ
"The Day when neither wealth nor children will benefit anyone — except one who comes to Allah with a sound heart." — Surah Ash-Shu'ara 26:88-89

Diseases of the Heart

Imam Al-Ghazali identified key spiritual diseases: arrogance (kibr), envy (hasad), love of the dunya, riya (showing off), and hatred. These diseases block the light of iman from entering the heart.

How to Purify the Heart

1. Constant Dhikr: The remembrance of Allah softens and purifies the heart. Start with Istighfar — 100 times a day, as the Prophet ﷺ himself did.

2. Reciting and Reflecting on the Quran: The Quran is shifa — a healing — for what is in the hearts. (Surah Yunus 10:57)

3. Guarding the Eyes and Tongue: Most heart diseases enter through what we see, hear, and say. Be mindful of your consumption.

4. Righteous Company: Ibn Al-Qayyim said: "Sitting with the righteous is medicine; sitting with the wicked is disease."

🤍 The journey of the heart is lifelong. Do not be discouraged by falls — the one who keeps returning to Allah has a heart still alive. May Allah grant us all Qalb-e-Saleem. Ameen.
